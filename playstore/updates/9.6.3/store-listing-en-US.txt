APP NAME
CineStream Local Video Player

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Private local video playback with broad codec recovery and smooth controls.

FULL DESCRIPTION
CineStream is a local video player built around your device library.

Browse videos stored on your device, search by title, switch between video and folder views, sort your library, and resume from where you stopped. Select one or multiple videos with clearly labeled Rename, Share, Info, and Delete actions.

Android asks for video access on first launch. If you deny permission, CineStream explains why it is needed and provides an Allow access button. When Android requires a settings change, Open settings takes you there. On Android 14 and newer, you can use selected-video access and Manage access to choose which videos appear.

Control playback with screen lock, pinch zoom, crop modes, gesture seeking, brightness and volume gestures, and temporary 2x speed. Choose available audio tracks and subtitles, and continue active playback in Android picture-in-picture with previous-video, play/pause, and next-video controls.

CineStream starts with Android hardware playback. For challenging media, it can use its bundled on-device software decoder and governed H.264 compatibility recovery. Playback capability depends on the source and the device's available resources.

FEATURES
- Local video and folder library
- Search, sorting, thumbnails, and playback resume
- Labeled selection actions and multiple-video sharing
- Hardware-first playback with on-device codec recovery
- Available audio-track and subtitle selection
- Picture-in-picture with playlist controls
- Pinch zoom, crop, seek, brightness, and volume gestures
- No account, advertising, analytics, or tracking
- No internet permission

Your videos stay on your device. CineStream processes media locally. Sharing happens only when you choose Share and a recipient through Android's system sharing interface.
