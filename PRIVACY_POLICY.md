# CineStream Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 7, 2026

CineStream is a local-first Android video player developed and maintained by Aditya Singh. This policy explains how CineStream handles information when you use the app.

## Data collection and sharing

CineStream does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties. The app does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking, account creation, or cloud-sync services, and it does not request Android's internet permission.

## Local media access

CineStream requests access to videos stored on your device so it can provide its core functions: displaying your video library, reading media metadata and thumbnails, playing videos, remembering playback progress, and performing user-requested rename, share, or delete actions.

Your videos remain on your device. Playback recovery and compatibility processing are performed on-device. CineStream accesses only media made available through Android's media and storage permission systems.

When you explicitly use Share, Android's system sharing interface sends the videos you selected to the app or service you choose. That recipient handles the shared content under its own privacy policy.

## Information stored on the device

CineStream may store playback positions, interface preferences, and locally generated compatibility-cache files in the app's private storage. This information is used only to provide playback and resume features. You can remove it through Android's Clear storage control or by uninstalling CineStream.

Renaming or deleting a video occurs only after a user action and, where required, Android's system confirmation.

## External links

The About screen can open the CineStream GitHub repository and this privacy policy in your chosen external browser. CineStream does not control the privacy practices of external websites or browser apps.

## Children's privacy

CineStream is not specifically designed for children and does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

## Changes to this policy

Material changes will be published at this same URL with an updated effective date.
